PROGRESSION LOGIC

Weeks 1–4 each use 50%, 65%, 70%, 75%, 80% and 85% of the overall target. If you manually alter a Target KG, the same kg increase or decrease is carried through all following sets in that week.

PRACTICAL LOADING

Target KG rounds to the nearest 1.5 kg. BB weights use the user-entered barbell weight: (Target KG − BB Weight) ÷ 2, rounded to the nearest 1.5 kg per side.
