SESSION NOTES

PROGRESSION LOGIC

Week 1 uses 50%, 65%, 70%, 75%, 80% and 85% of the overall target. Weeks 2–4 start Set 1 at the previous week’s Set 2 percentage; Sets 2–6 then rise by 5 percentage points.

PRACTICAL LOADING

Target KG rounds to the nearest 1.5 kg. BB weights use the user-entered barbell weight: (Target KG − BB Weight) ÷ 2, rounded to the nearest 1.5 kg per side.
